Data sources for scene.html (all openly licensed):

- Brain, brainstem, cerebellum, vertebrae, spinal cord canal meshes:
  BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS),
  licensed under CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan.
  Via github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D

- Neuron morphology (cnic_001): NeuroMorpho.org (Wearne_Hof archive),
  a reconstruction from light microscopy. Freely available for research/education.

- Myelinated axon: built to a labelled anatomy reference (not downloaded);
  a regular structure specified by textbook cross-section + longitudinal diagrams.
